Inside the build

Three decisions you can feel in the first ten seconds.

One gold key, nothing else

Gold is spent twice per screen at most — the chosen tier and the trial button. Everything else is ivory on graphite, so the one warm thing on the page is the one thing to tap.

Reels that land, not drift

The feed uses mandatory vertical scroll-snap with a hard stop, so a flick always settles on one whole story instead of parking between two.

Every still keeps its frame

Posters run at their own aspect ratio and reels are letterboxed on emerald-black. A 3:1 crescent and a 3:4 courtyard share a row without either one being cropped.
